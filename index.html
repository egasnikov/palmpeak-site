Ways to Invest

Two paths, one standard

Whether you are an individual investor, a family office, a wealth manager or an institution, participate through the structure that fits you.

Option I

Deal-by-Deal

Full ownership and transparency. Each property is registered in the investor's name, with profits calculated and distributed per asset after sale.

  • Property selection and purchase in your name
  • Renovation managed end-to-end by Palm & Peak
  • Sale, closing and return per individual asset
Option II

The Fund

Capital under management. Our team runs the entire property lifecycle, acquisition through sale, with dividends distributed every six months.

  • Single entry, diversified across projects
  • Full lifecycle managed by the fund's team
  • Dividends every 6 months on fund performance
